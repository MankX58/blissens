## Brand & Style

The design system projects clinical excellence, refined precision, and serene reassurance. Designed for high-tier aesthetic and general dentistry, the interface eliminates clinical apprehension through luminous, pristine whites, deep structural navy accents, and sophisticated medical teal. 

The aesthetic is Modern Clinical Minimalist with subtle tactile elevation. It rejects harsh medical coldness in favor of airy negative space, immaculate typographic hierarchies, and functional, high-clarity status signifiers. Visual weight prioritizes clinical hygiene, patient trust, and friction-free operational efficiency.

## Colors

The palette balances serene clinical professionalism with decisive operational tracking:

- **Primary (`#0D9488` / `#14B8A6`)**: Used for key interactive actions, primary patient status indicators, and confident brand touchpoints.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`)**: Clinical deep navy anchors structural headers, high-emphasis text, and core navigation frames.
- **Neutral Foundations**: Surfaces default to pure `#FFFFFF` over `#F8FAFC` base canvases, with `#F1F5F9` providing soft structural divisions. Slate (`#64748B`) governs secondary metadata.
- **Status & Tracking System**:
  - *Active / Success*: Emerald (`#10B981`) paired with subtle tinted surfaces (`#ECFDF5`).
  - *7-Day Alert / Warning*: Amber (`#F59E0B`) with calm parchment backings (`#FFFBEB`).
  - *15-Day Urgent / Critical*: Crimson (`#EF4444`) with soft alerting backgrounds (`#FEF2F2`).
- **Direct Engagement**: WhatsApp corporate green (`#25D366`) is preserved exclusively for direct patient messaging channels and emergency contact CTA buttons.

## Typography

The type system is powered uniformly by Plus Jakarta Sans. Its modern geometric curves with subtle humanist touches soften clinical terminology while preserving legibility in complex appointment tables, charts, and odontograms.

- **Headings**: Use negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) with medium-to-bold weights in deep navy (`#0F172A`) to project confidence and structure.
- **Body Text**: Tuned for maximum readability with generous line heights (`1.45` to `1.6`) in balanced neutral tones (`#334155`).
- **Labels & Badges**: Set with higher contrast weights (600/700) and slight positive tracking for clinical status clarity at miniature scale.

## Layout & Spacing

The grid utilizes a structured 12-column layout on desktop (max-width `1280px` centered), collapsing to 6 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile viewports.

Rhythm and breathing space reinforce sanitization and clinical order. Content density scales by context: patient medical records and appointment grids use compact `space-sm` and `space-md` gaps, while patient facing consultation summaries and onboarding interfaces expand using `space-lg` and `space-xl` paddings to avoid overwhelming the user.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp, ultra-soft diffused shadows tinted with clinical slate (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` to `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`), entirely avoiding muddy grayscale shadows. 

- **Level 0 (Base)**: `#F8FAFC` background canvas.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` fill supported by a hairline boundary `1px solid #E2E8F0` and `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns & Active Overlays)**: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Alerts)**: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Level 4 (Direct Action Hover)**: Subtle teal-tinted ambient glow `0 10px 20px -3px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced roundedness (Level 2), where standard UI elements utilize `0.5rem` (8px) radius, structured containers feature `1rem` (16px), and cards reach `1.5rem` (24px). Status badges and direct action floating buttons employ continuous pill geometries (`9999px`) to create distinction from rigid clinical data tables.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#0D9488` with `#FFFFFF` text, subtle hover shift to `#14B8A6`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Border radius: `0.5rem`.
- **Secondary**: `#F1F5F9` background, `#0F172A` text, hover to `#E2E8F0`.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action**: `#25D366` surface with `#FFFFFF` text and accompanying brand icon. Hover: subtle elevation lift with `rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.25)` ambient shadow.

### Status Badges & Chips (Semáforo de Seguimiento)
Pill-shaped containers (`padding: 0.25rem 0.75rem`, font: `label-sm`), pairing a tinted background with a high-contrast label and an embedded 6px circular indicator dot:
- **Active / Control al día**: Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, dot `#10B981`.
- **Alerta 7 días**: Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, dot `#F59E0B`.
- **Urgente 15 días / Crítico**: Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, dot `#EF4444`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Crisp white surface, `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, `0.5rem` radius. Focused state applies `#0D9488` border with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)` optical ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom circular and soft-square geometries with `#0D9488` fill on checked state, providing high-contrast confirmation tickmarks.

### Cards & Clinical Summaries
- Encased in `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `1rem` internal padding, and Level 1 elevation.
- Critical patient records incorporate a subtle 3px left border accent corresponding to their triage color (Emerald, Amber, or Red).
